      How this calculator works

      Each month, interest is added to every debt at its yearly rate ÷ 12. Minimum payments go to every debt, and whatever is left of your budget goes to one target debt. When a debt is cleared, its payment rolls on to the next target.

      • Avalanche: target the highest interest rate first. This always costs the least interest.
      • Snowball: target the smallest balance first. You clear debts sooner, which helps some people stay motivated.

      Assumptions: rates stay fixed, no new spending on the cards, no fees.

      Frequently asked questions

      Which method should I choose?

      If the interest difference is small, pick the one you'll stick with. If avalanche saves a lot, it's usually worth it.

      What if my budget doesn't cover the minimums?

      The calculator will tell you. Contact your lenders early: many offer hardship plans, and free debt advice services exist in most countries.

      Is my data stored?

      No. Everything is calculated in your browser.
